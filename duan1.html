Strange cloud phenomena

Summary

  1. Lenticular Clouds
  2. Mammatus Clouds
  3. Asperitas Clouds
  1. Strange clouds are a rare and fascinating meteorological phenomenon that can take many forms, from lenticular clouds to mammatus clouds.
  2. Lenticular clouds are lens-shaped clouds that form over mountains or other topographical features, often appearing to hover in the sky.
  3. Mammatus clouds are pouch-like formations that hang from the base of a cloud, often associated with severe thunderstorms.
  4. Other types of strange clouds include roll clouds, Kelvin-Helmholtz clouds, and noctilucent clouds, each with their own unique characteristics and formation processes.

What Are Strange Clouds?

Strange clouds are unusual or rare cloud formations that do not fit into the ten basic cloud categories taught in school. They often feature dramatic shapes, striking colors, or unusual physical mechanics caused by extreme atmospheric conditions, wind shear, or unique altitudes
